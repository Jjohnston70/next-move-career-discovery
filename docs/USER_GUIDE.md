# User guide — Next Move

## Purpose
Explore what kinds of work interest you and how to test possible directions. This is not an exam. Answers are yours; there are no right or wrong answers.

## Getting started

1. Extract the ZIP and open `Next-Move.html` in Chrome, Safari or Edge.
2. Choose the **Read this first** section before answering anything.
3. Decide whether to opt in to local device saving. If you leave it off, closing the tab can lose your progress; make a user-initiated backup if you want to keep answers.
4. Work through the six discovery sections and the separate **O*NET interest profile**. You can stop and return at any point if you have a saved backup.
5. Use **Career possibilities** to see multiple options. The examples are ideas, not automatic job assignments.
6. Use **Real-world tests** to record interviews, shadowing or other low-risk activities you choose. Do not attempt safety-critical tasks without the training and authorization they require.
7. Open **My field guide** for a personal report. Decide whether to download, print, or share anything.
8. Under **Finish & share**, preview the selected report and confirm if you choose to prepare an email. You must press **Send** in your own email app. Full reports require a manual attachment.

## O*NET section
The embedded 30-item Mini Interest Profiler is the **published 2016 version**. Its six RIASEC results describe interests, not aptitude, diagnoses or guaranteed career fit. The current official assessment is available at https://onetinterestprofiler.org/ .

For optional occupation data, follow the official links inside the app, save the three selected JSON files and import them using the file picker. There is no background API connection or automatic dataset update.

## Protect your privacy
Avoid typing identification numbers, passwords, medical information, or confidential employer records. Do not upload exported reports to public issues or a public repository. Device storage is not encrypted. If you share a report, review its content first.

For general help read the in-app **Read this first** and **Privacy & backups** screens.
