# Community conduct

Next Move is intended to help people explore work and careers without shame,
pressure, assumptions, or discrimination. Everyone participating in this
repository should communicate respectfully and protect personal information.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without making personal attacks.
- Respect different work histories, interests, education levels, and priorities.
- Provide constructive, specific feedback that another person can use.
- Use synthetic examples when reporting an issue or testing code.
- Follow the project's source and O*NET licensing boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, or sustained personal targeting.
- Posting someone else's assessment, reflections, contact details, or secrets.
- Misrepresenting Next Move as an official hiring test, diagnosis, or government
  endorsed assessment.

## Reports and enforcement

Report community-conduct concerns privately using the maintainer's public
business contact listed in the project documentation. Do not disclose private
participant details in public issues. Maintainers may remove harmful content,
close issues, and restrict participation to protect users and contributors.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, and pull requests.
